/* ABOUT */

NVO987 Cryptographic & Digital Infrastructure is a collection of
independent cryptographic software applications and digital tools.

The project is maintained under the NVO987 namespace.

Applications include hash utilities, data-format tools,
identifier generators, and related web-based software.
